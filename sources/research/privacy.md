# Research data privacy safeguards

A local language-model backend can reduce data sharing with external providers, but logs, telemetry, model downloads, user documents, and endpoint configuration still require review. Do not upload confidential research participant records or client proprietary text to a hosted model without permission.

Before indexing a research corpus, inspect its license and the conditions under which the documents may be copied, redistributed, or quoted. A file fingerprint verifies bytes and does not establish intellectual-property permission or the correctness of research claims.
